About Us

A cake is a sweet baked food typically made from flour, sugar, eggs, and a leavening agent, and often includes fats, flavorings, and other ingredients like fruits or nuts. Cakes are a diverse category of baked goods, with ancient origins that evolved from bread-like foods into the modern desserts enjoyed today for celebrations and everyday occasions.
